About Me

I'm a performance marketer who's spent 6+ years managing paid media campaigns across Meta, Google, LinkedIn, TikTok, and Snapchat — delivering measurable results for clients in real estate, eCommerce, education, B2B energy, and home services.

I specialize in full-funnel campaign strategy — from cold audience prospecting to retargeting and conversion optimization. My approach combines platform expertise with AI-assisted workflows (Claude, ChatGPT, Make.com, Looker Studio) to operate more efficiently and deliver cleaner insights to clients.

I've managed ₱5M+ in total ad spend, working remotely with clients in the Philippines, Australia, and the United States. I've worked with everyone from local entrepreneurs to growing startups and established brands — always with the same focus: clear KPIs, weekly reporting, and continuous optimization.

Beyond performance marketing, I'm a Certified Paralegal and current Juris Doctor student. This legal background gives me a unique edge when working with compliance-sensitive industries — I understand both the performance metrics and the regulatory constraints.

Every campaign I run is built around measurable outcomes. I'm not interested in vanity metrics or "awareness" plays — I optimize for conversions, leads, and revenue. I use AI tools to move faster, but the strategy, judgment, and optimization are manual. The results speak for themselves.

Currently open to remote performance marketing roles — particularly those in legal, fintech, healthcare, or other regulated industries where my background adds strategic value.
